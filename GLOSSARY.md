# Lumina

Bible passage selection and projection during gatherings.

## Language

**Operator**:
The person selecting Bible content and controlling what the audience sees.
_Avoid_: Presenter, when referring to the person operating Lumina

**Operator window**:
The operator's private workspace for finding Bible content and controlling projection.
_Avoid_: Main screen, control screen

**Audience window**:
The presentation surface containing the Bible content intended for the audience.
_Avoid_: Operator screen, reading window

**Cursor restriction**:
Preventing the mouse pointer from entering the audience window.
_Avoid_: Cursor hiding, when referring to restricting movement

**Translation**:
A named Bible text used for searching and projection.
_Avoid_: Version, when it could mean an application release

**Translation edition**:
A particular text of a translation, distinguished from a later replacement.

**Saved copy**:
A verse's original recorded wording and translation label retained in verse history.
_Avoid_: Current text, when referring to an earlier recorded edition

**Book identity**:
The Bible book being referred to across translations, independently of its localized name or position in a book list.
_Avoid_: Book number, when the numbering convention is unspecified

**Verse queue**:
An ordered collection of verses prepared for projection during a gathering.
_Avoid_: Playlist, setlist

**Projection history**:
The part of verse history containing verses sent to the audience, including the translation used.
_Avoid_: Search history, viewed-verse history

**Verse history**:
A record of verses selected for preview or sent to the audience, including their translations.
_Avoid_: Search history

**Preview**:
Bible content selected for inspection before it is sent to the audience.
_Avoid_: Live preview, when referring to content that has not been projected

**Live preview**:
A representation of the content currently shown in the audience window.
_Avoid_: Preview, when referring to content selected for inspection

**Quick reference**:
Direct entry of a Bible book, chapter, and verse for projection.
_Avoid_: Word search

**Book information**:
Supplemental information about a Bible book, including its name, author, date, and writing location.

**Size band**:
A range of verse-text lengths sharing the same audience font-size setting.

**Search mode**:
A named rule determining how entered words or text match Bible verses.
_Avoid_: Search scope, when referring to matching behavior

**Search scope**:
The set of Bible books within which a word search runs.
_Avoid_: Search mode, when referring to which books are searched
