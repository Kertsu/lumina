# Lumina

Bible passage selection and projection during gatherings.

## Language

**Operator**:
The person selecting Bible content and controlling what the audience sees.
_Avoid_: Presenter, when referring to the person operating Lumina

**Operator window**:
The application window containing the operator's controls for finding Bible content and managing projection.
_Avoid_: Main screen, control screen

**Operator workspace**:
An independent Bible selection and projection context associated with one audience window.
_Avoid_: Operator window, when referring to the context rather than its application window

**Operator tab**:
The selectable representation of one operator workspace within the operator window.
_Avoid_: Audience window, when referring to the workspace's controls

**Audience window**:
The presentation surface containing the Bible content intended for the audience.
_Avoid_: Operator screen, reading window

**Application theme**:
A paired light and dark color palette shared by Lumina's operator and audience windows.
_Avoid_: Workspace appearance, when referring to the shared application palette

**Cursor hiding**:
Suppressing the pointer image over an audience window while leaving pointer movement and input possible.
_Avoid_: Pointer confinement, when referring only to visibility

**Translation**:
A named Bible text used for searching and projection.
_Avoid_: Version, when it could mean an application release

**Bible library**:
The installed Bible translations available to all operator workspaces.
_Avoid_: Selected translation, when referring to the shared collection

**Translation edition**:
A particular text of a translation, distinguished from a later replacement.

**Saved copy**:
Captured verse text or book information retained with its translation label when its source edition is no longer available.
_Avoid_: Current text, when referring to an earlier recorded edition

**Book identity**:
The Bible book being referred to across translations, independently of its localized name or position in a book list.
_Avoid_: Book number, when the numbering convention is unspecified

**Versification**:
A translation edition's assignment of Scripture content to chapters and verses.
_Avoid_: Book numbering, when referring to chapter/verse divisions

**Verse coordinate**:
A book, chapter, and verse identifying a location within a particular translation edition.
_Avoid_: Universal passage identity, when referring only to matching reference numbers

**Projection history**:
The part of verse history containing verses whose application was confirmed by the intended audience window, including the translation used.
_Avoid_: Search history, viewed-verse history

**Unconfirmed projection**:
A requested audience change whose application has not been confirmed, leaving whether it appeared uncertain.
_Avoid_: Failed projection, when delivery is uncertain

**Verse history**:
A record of verses selected for display or sent to the audience, including their translations.
_Avoid_: Search history

**Current verse**:
The Bible verse selected for an operator workspace, retained while book information is displayed in its place.
_Avoid_: Verse preview, when referring to the selected display content

**Verse preview**:
A candidate verse inspected privately, including its reference and translation, before the operator chooses whether to send it to the audience.
_Avoid_: Current verse, when the inspected candidate has not been selected for display

**Quick reference**:
Direct entry of a Bible book, chapter, and verse identifying content for projection or private inspection.
_Avoid_: Word search

**Book information**:
Supplemental information about a Bible book, including its name, author, date, and writing location.

**Book information mode**:
An audience presentation of book information in place of the workspace's retained current verse.

**Size band**:
A range of verse-text lengths sharing the same audience font-size setting.

**Search mode**:
A named rule determining how entered words or text match Bible verses.
_Avoid_: Search scope, when referring to matching behavior

**Search scope**:
The set of Bible books within which a word search runs.
_Avoid_: Search mode, when referring to which books are searched
